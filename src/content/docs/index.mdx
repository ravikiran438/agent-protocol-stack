## Transports

These are governance extensions, not a transport of their own. They bind to the
agent stack's transports: **A2A** (agent&#8596;agent) via `capabilities.extensions[]`
and **MCP** (agent&#8596;tools) via `_meta`. They also bind to
[**AG-UI**](https://github.com/ag-ui-protocol/ag-ui), the agent&#8596;human
transport — the edge where a person enters a run to grant consent, respond to a
capacity check, or rate an outcome.
